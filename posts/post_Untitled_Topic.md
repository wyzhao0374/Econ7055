AI is changing classrooms.
Artificial intelligence is being used to personalize learning for students.
This approach is showing promising results in improving student outcomes.

The EducAItion initiative is a notable example of this trend.
It aims to bring AI-powered tools to the classroom to enhance the learning experience.

Most education systems have not invested enough in teacher training for AI adoption.
This lack of training can hinder the effective use of AI tools in schools.

AI can help teachers automate grading and focus on more critical tasks.
Teachers need to be trained to effectively use these tools to improve student learning.
